The Meta-Layer Initiative: Call for Input

We need your voice in shaping the future of the decentralized web.

The 2024 election marks a pivotal moment to rebuild a safer, more collaborative internet that champions peace, freedom, and sovereignty. Amid rising concerns over media manipulation and digital rights, we face an urgent need to safeguard privacy, free speech, and democratic integrity in an increasingly AI-driven world.

Together, we can create a secure and authentic digital space within the decentralized web, where transparency reigns, manipulation is constrained, and democracy thrives for generations to come. Will you join us in re-envisioning the web as a medium of communication and collaboration?

Introducing the Meta-Layer initiative, an exciting effort to catalyze the next evolution of the web. Imagine a layer of interaction above any webpage where smart tags attach actionable insights and meta-communities allow like-minded individuals to gather, share knowledge, and collaborate safely. This isn't just about improving the web—it's about building a human-centered, decentralized space for collective sensemaking and problem-solving.

With the guidance of Vint Cerf, a “Father of the Internet,” we've begun defining the properties of this transformative meta-layer. Now, we're turning to you—the community—to help shape this vision. Your input will ensure that the Meta-Layer reflects the diverse needs and aspirations of its stakeholders.

As a token of our appreciation, participants will receive a digital Overweb badge—a dynamic Bitcoin NFT (BRC333 protocol) that evolves with your contributions. This badge is more than recognition; it's a living record of your role in shaping the web's future.

Join us in building a safe, decentralized, and human-centered digital future. Your voice matters, and your contributions will be preserved on the Bitcoin blockchain for posterity. Learn how to participate in the Call for Input today and help create a web that empowers everyone.
